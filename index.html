How to use these

  • On a phone — it opens straight into full-screen app mode. Tap buttons, cards and the bottom tabs to move between screens; your phone's back gesture works.
  • On a computer — you'll see all 24 screens laid out at once. Hover any phone and click to start tapping through it.
  • Jump anywhere — the grid button on the right edge opens a list of every screen, handy during a walkthrough.
  • Light or dark — Clinical Blue follows your device setting, and you can switch it in Profile → Dark mode.
