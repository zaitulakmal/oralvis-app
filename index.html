3
Hold steady
Stay still for three seconds while the scanner sweeps each arch.
4
Read your report

Conditions of Use
warning
ORAVIU is not a diagnosis. Results are indicative only and must not replace examination by a qualified dental professional.
1 · Intended use
ORAVIU captures intraoral images and applies an AI model to flag visible signs such as calculus, stain and early decay.
2 · Limitations
The model reads only what the camera can see. It cannot assess anything below the gum line, between contact points, or inside the tooth. A clear result does not rule out disease.
3 · Your data
Scans are stored against your account so you can track change over time. You may delete any scan from Profile.
4 · Seeking care
If you have pain, bleeding, swelling or a visible change that concerns you, contact a dentist regardless of what ORAVIU reports.
